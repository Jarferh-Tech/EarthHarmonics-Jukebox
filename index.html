NASA Earth Information Jukebox Guide

NASA's Earth Information Center (EIC) tracks our changing planet across space and time. EarthHarmonics sonifies these changes so you can experience them through your ears:


1. Atmospheric CO₂: As carbon dioxide rises from 338 ppm to over 426 ppm, the pitch climbs upward continuously over nearly 2 octaves.


2. Global Temperature Anomaly: Cold anomalies produce smooth consonance. As temperatures surge past +1.0°C in the 21st century, the synth adds detuned saw harmonics and tremolo agitation.


3. Arctic Sea Ice Decline: The audio begins with bright, chiming bells representing thick multi-year ice, then dampens and descends into deep ambient bass as polar sea ice shrinks.
